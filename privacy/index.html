Privacy Policy

Sofa RC ("the app") is a remote control for Android TV and Google TV devices, developed by Jetra Apps ("we"). This policy explains what data the app uses and why. We do not run any server for the app: we do not collect, receive, store or sell your personal data, except what you choose to send us through the feedback form (see below).

Data stored only on your device

The paired TVs (name and local IP address), a security certificate used to pair with your TVs, your preferences (dictation language, touchpad mode) and whether you have Pro. This data is only shared with your own TVs over your local network and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Local network

The app finds TVs on your Wi‑Fi network (mDNS/Bonjour) and talks to them directly over your local network to send commands. This traffic does not go through the internet or to us.

Microphone and speech recognition

Only when you tap a voice button. Dictation uses your phone's speech recognition (Apple or Google), which may process the audio according to their own privacy policies; the resulting text is sent to your TV. Assistant sends the audio directly to your TV's Google Assistant. We never receive or store audio or text.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, approximate location and ad interactions to show, measure and, with your consent, personalize ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent, and on iOS you can allow or deny tracking. You can change your choice at any time from the "Ad privacy" option in the app. See how Google uses data. The Pro version shows no ads.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by the App Store or Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. Promotional codes are verified on your device.

Feedback form

If you use our feedback form, we store the email, subject and comment you enter, plus the date and language, in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). We only use them to reply to you and improve the app, never for marketing, and we do not share them. Ask us at any time to delete them.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

We will publish any changes on this page. Questions: jetra.apps@gmail.com.
